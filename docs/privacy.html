Tomi Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Version 1.3 · Applies to Tomi for Android

Tomi is an offline-first movement, task, and journal app for people who work at a desk. This policy explains what data Tomi handles, why it is handled, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Tomi is developed and published by @okyssta. Privacy or security questions can be reported through the repository's private vulnerability reporting channel.

Data stored on your device

  • Tasks, due dates and times, notes, categories, priorities, and completion state.
  • Journal titles, text, tags, mood, and pinned state.
  • Movement sessions such as walks, stretches, standing breaks, and push-up sets.
  • Daily mood and energy check-ins.
  • Water entries and daily goals.
  • Step totals cached from Health Connect for display in Tomi.
  • Prayer tracker entries and the optional approximate location used to calculate prayer times.
  • Reminder, appearance, goal, and update preferences.

This data is stored in Tomi's app-private Room database and DataStore files. It is not uploaded to a Tomi server. Android cloud backup and device-transfer extraction are disabled; Tomi provides explicit JSON export and import instead, plus optional user-selected Google Drive backup.

Health Connect

With your permission, Tomi reads aggregated step totals through Android Health Connect. Step data is used to show daily and weekly progress and, only if you separately allow background access, to reset the movement reminder after Tomi detects walking.

  • READ_STEPS is used for foreground step display and smart pause.
  • READ_HEALTH_DATA_IN_BACKGROUND is optional and used only for closed-app smart pause and widget refreshes.
  • Google Fit data can be included when Google Fit is connected to Health Connect.
  • Tomi does not request write access and does not create, modify, or delete Health Connect records.
  • Tomi does not read location, routes, heart rate, weight, or other health record types.

Health Connect source records remain controlled by Health Connect and their original apps. Clearing Tomi does not delete those source records.

Network access

The Play Store edition does not include Tomi's APK self-updater. The direct-download edition can contact GitHub to check for a new Tomi release and download a published APK. It verifies the download against the release's SHA-256 checksum, and Android verifies the APK signature before installation. Installation always requires your confirmation.

Tomi does not operate any analytics, advertising, account, or cloud-sync service. It does not transmit tasks, journal text, mood, water, movement history, or cached step totals to a Tomi server.

Optional Google Drive backup

Tomi can optionally write a JSON backup to a folder you select through Android's system document picker. This uses Android's Storage Access Framework, not a Google sign-in SDK or a Tomi account. Tomi receives read/write access only to the folder you choose; it does not receive your Google password or OAuth token.

The backup contains the records listed under “Data stored on your device.” It is written directly to your chosen provider at Tomi Backups/tomi-backup.json, overwritten on each sync, and controlled by your Google Drive account. Tomi never uploads it to a Tomi server. Disconnecting in Settings releases the folder grant; the existing file remains in your Drive until you delete it. Restoring a Drive backup replaces local Tomi data after confirmation.

Namaz addon and location

The optional Namaz addon calculates prayer times on-device using the Adhan library and your approximate location. Tomi requests ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION only when you tap “Use current location”; the permission is not required for any other feature. The latitude and longitude are stored only in Tomi’s local settings to recalculate times; they are not uploaded to a Tomi server. Prayer offered/not-offered entries are stored locally and are included in your JSON or Drive backup.

Permissions

  • Notifications: movement alarms and, in the direct edition, update notices.
  • Exact alarms: optional; improves reminder timing. Inexact alarms are used when it is not granted.
  • Boot completed: restores reminder scheduling after restart, app replacement, clock changes, or timezone changes.
  • Vibration: optional movement-alarm feedback.
  • Approximate location: optional Namaz addon only; used on-device for prayer times.
  • Install unknown apps: direct edition only; required solely for in-app APK updates.
  • Internet: direct edition only; release checks and downloads.

Retention, deletion, and export

Tomi retains local data until you delete individual items, import a replacement backup, clear all Tomi data, or uninstall the app. Settings → Data & privacy → Clear all data deletes the local database, preferences, reminder schedule, pending update work, notifications, and downloaded update files.

Files you export yourself are controlled by you and are not deleted by Tomi. Health Connect source records are outside Tomi's local deletion scope. You can revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings or Health Connect.

Security

Tomi uses Android app-private storage, parameterized Room queries, bounded backup imports, read-only Health Connect access, HTTPS release downloads, checksum verification, and Android package-signature verification. No security system is perfect; report suspected vulnerabilities privately through GitHub.

Children

Tomi is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used to collect health data from children.
